  Halloween Dinner; their text remains in ignored local output only.
- Operation :: Local Pandoc DOCX-to-ODT, then local LibreOffice PDF rendering.
- Evidence :: Compare page size/count, text, table/cell structures, styles,
  running matter and representative pages against the DOCX previews.

  needed. No hosted service, network submission or metered API; no persistent
  regression target and no ODT CLI support added by this experiment.
- Decision :: Recommend whether stock Pandoc conversion meets the agreed
  formatting/editability intent; document losses and keep a contrary result.
