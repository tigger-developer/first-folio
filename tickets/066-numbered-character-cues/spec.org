** Outcome
- Org script exports *retain numeral components in character names* and *separate parenthesized directions*.
** Before
- =**** PARENT 3= *split the 3 into a direction* and malformed the output cue.
- =**** MARY (IGNORING the question)= *included the uppercase instruction in the name*.
** After
- =PARENT 3= and other numbered names *render as complete cues*; a following parenthetical *remains a direction regardless of capitalization*.
** Changes
- The shared Org speaker parser *accepts numeral tokens within a name* and *recognizes an opening parenthesis as a direction boundary*.
** Unchanged
- Lowercase delivery instructions *remain directions*; other document modes and output writers are unchanged.
** Edge cases
- *Multiple digits* remain in the name; both lowercase and uppercase parenthesized instructions *remain separate*.
** Decisions
- The change belongs in the *shared event parser*, before PDF, DOCX and ODT rendering.
** Next step
- Hand the checked candidate to the operator for the *closure decision*.

* Edge Cases
- A *multi-digit numeral* remains one part of the character name.
- A *parenthesized lowercase phrase* still starts a direction after a numbered name.
- A *parenthesized uppercase phrase* starts a direction after numbered and unnumbered names.
- An *ordinary lowercase instruction* still starts a direction after an unnumbered name.

* Solution Design

** Shared Org speaker parser
- In =parseCharacterLine=, continue the name across tokens whose initial rune is uppercase or a Unicode digit. An opening parenthesis explicitly starts the direction before that case check; otherwise, the first later token with neither property begins the direction as before. The existing event and renderer interfaces do not change.

** Security and operational impact
- No new dependency, external access, persistence, or unbounded parsing step is introduced. Existing conversion and output error handling remain.
