## Original issue body

## Problem

Manuscript body paragraphs render without an explicit justification policy. Authors cannot select justified or ragged-right body text through `script.yaml`.

## Solution

Add `folio.manuscript.justify` as a boolean manuscript setting. The British base preset defaults it to `true`, and the US override inherits that value. Explicit `false` emits ragged-right body paragraphs. The setting applies to the running manuscript body paragraph rule and does not alter title-page or table-of-contents composition. Document the setting alongside line spacing and paragraph geometry.
